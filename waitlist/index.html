FAQs

Common questions

Do I need experience in property?

No. We work with first-time investors and experienced portfolio builders alike. Our role is to do the technical work — you just need capital and clarity on your goals.

What's the minimum investment?

This depends on the strategy and location. Our deals typically start from around £50,000 for a cash purchase. We'll discuss what's appropriate on your strategy call.

How do you make money?

We charge a sourcing fee per deal, which is disclosed upfront before you commit. There are no hidden charges.

Am I obligated to buy once I join?

Not at all. Joining the waitlist is free and commitment-free. You review deals when they're presented and decide if and when you want to proceed.

What areas do you source in?

We focus on high-yield UK residential markets including Wigan, Leeds, Burnley, Grimsby, Skegness, and Huddersfield, with additional locations added as sourcing briefs are completed.

What happens after I join the waitlist?
