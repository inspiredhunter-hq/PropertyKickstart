Landlord Register Readiness Workbook

A free workbook to get your property, tenancy and compliance information organised before England’s new rental property register opens.

Regulations still being finalised. The Private Rented Sector Database Regulations 2026 are in draft (status checked 11 September 2026). This is a preparation tool, not legal advice. Re-check the final rules before you register.

What it helps you organise

  • Landlord detailsIndividual, company or other ownership entity, contacts and managing agent.
  • One row per propertyAddress, region, tenancy or occupancy type, rent and utilities.
  • LicensingHMO, additional and selective licence numbers and expiry dates.
  • Safety and energy evidenceGas safety, EICR and EPC dates, with links to your documents.
  • Exemptions and scope checksMEES exemptions and the property types that need checking.
  • Your deadlinesYour region’s registration window, readiness status and next actions.

How it works

  1. Enter your first name and email.
  2. Click Make my workbook copy. Google saves your own copy to your Drive (you’ll need a free Google account).
  3. We also email you the link, so you can come back to it later.

It’s your copy, so you can update it as you prepare. The master stays locked, and nothing you type into your copy is shared with us.
